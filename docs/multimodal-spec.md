# Multimodal content specification

Generated by `node tools/report-multimodal.mjs` from the syllabus content and the overlays. Regenerate after changing either.

## Principles

1. **Text is the baseline.** Every lesson is complete in the *Read* mode, which cannot be switched off.
2. **Modes are additive and optional.** *See*, *Hear* and *Do* add layers; learners can combine or switch them at any time from the bar at the top of each lesson. The app never labels a learner as a "type".
3. **Every non-text item has a text equivalent.** Charts have a text description and a data table; audio has a transcript; animations are captioned with the syllabus steps; drag activities have button or menu alternatives.
4. **AI involvement is disclosed.** Synthetic voices and AI-assisted diagrams are labelled where they appear and on the AI-use page.
5. **Nothing heavy loads by default.** Audio and the Python runtime download only on request.

## What every module gets automatically

| Mode | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Read | Why it matters, objectives (with cognitive level), subtopics, key terms with first-use tooltips, core formulas, worked examples in Tracks A/B/C, cases, tasks, references and review date; printable notes | Syllabus (extracted) |
| See | Captioned step-through walkthrough of every Track A calculation with numbered steps | Syllabus Track A steps |
| Hear | Read-aloud of the whole page (on-device voice, 0.75×–2× speed, paragraph skip); downloadable audio summary with transcript | Syllabus text; Piper TTS |
| Do | Key-term matching game; spreadsheet pack of the module's Track B formulas; in-browser Python runner for Track C; knowledge check with randomised numeric items | Syllabus terms, formulas and code |

## Video

The brief asks for short captioned video or animation. v1 ships **captioned step-through animations** generated from each worked example's steps (no video files), because they are tiny, work offline, follow the learner's pace and respect reduced-motion settings. Recorded video is a candidate for v2 (see the content-update guide for captioning requirements).
